To manage resturant orders eficiently in the order they arive, 
a queue is ideal since it follows FIFO. 
Each order contain items with quantites. 
Use enqueue(order) to add new orders at rear and dequeue() to remove from the front. 
To prevent overflo, use dynamic memory or check with isFull(); 
to avoid underflow, use isEmpty(). As orders are processd, 
store them seperately and use printProcessed() to display all in original order for smooth fulfillment.
