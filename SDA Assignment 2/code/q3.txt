@startuml
|Customer Service Clerk|
start
:Enter customer information;
|System|
:Find policy and display details;
|Customer Service Clerk|
:Verify policy;
if (Policy is current?) then (no)
  :Request payment/Collect info;
else (yes)
endif

|Customer Service Clerk|
:Enter vehicle identification;
|System|
if (Car has known standard?) then (no)
  |Customer Service Clerk|
  :Refer to underwriting;
  stop
else (yes)
  :Validate car standard;
endif

|Customer Service Clerk|
repeat
  :Enter coverage type/amount;
  |System|
  :Validate coverage;
backward:Ask for changed amount;
repeat while (Coverage in range?) is (no)

|Customer Service Clerk|
:Indicate coverages complete;
|System|
if (Combination valid?) then (no)
  |Customer Service Clerk|
  goto repeat
else (yes)
  :Combination validation;
endif

|Customer Service Clerk|
:Invoke "Add New Person" (if needed);
:Change driver percentages;
|System|
:Update driver information;

|Customer Service Clerk|
:Indicate everything is complete;
|System|
:Calculate new premium;
:Print new statement;
stop
@enduml
